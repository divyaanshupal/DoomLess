Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 17, 2026

Thank you for using our application ("App"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the App collects, uses, and protects information.

1. Information We Collect

The App is designed to help users monitor and manage their short-form video consumption, including Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts activity.

The App may collect:

  • Reels scroll count
  • Shorts scroll count
  • App usage statistics related to supported applications
  • User preferences and settings

The App does not collect personal information such as:

  • Passwords
  • Messages
  • Email content
  • Photos or videos
  • Financial information
  • Contacts

2. Accessibility Service Usage

This App uses Android Accessibility Service solely for the purpose of detecting user interactions within supported applications in order to:

  • Count Instagram Reels scrolling activity
  • Count YouTube Shorts scrolling activity
  • Generate usage statistics
  • Provide screen-time management features
  • Enable future productivity and digital well-being features

Accessibility permissions are used only for these stated purposes and are not used to read, collect, store, or share personal content.

3. Data Storage

Usage statistics and scroll counts are stored locally on the user's device unless explicitly stated otherwise within the App.

We do not sell, rent, or trade user data to third parties.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share personal information or usage data with third parties except:

  • When required by law.
  • To protect user safety or legal rights.
  • When the user explicitly consents.

5. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored by the App. However, no method of electronic storage is completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. User Control

Users may revoke Accessibility permission at any time through their Android device settings.

Users may also uninstall the App to remove locally stored data.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page along with an updated effective date.
